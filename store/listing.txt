Full description (4000 chars max):
Naija Road Rush is an arcade racing game set on the busy roads of Nigeria.

Speed across six cities, each with its own landmarks, weather and traffic:
• LAGOS – Cross the Third Mainland Bridge, pass the Lekki Toll Plaza and weave between danfos and BRT buses.
• ABUJA – Race under Zuma Rock and Aso Rock through the Federal Capital's wide boulevards.
• PORT HARCOURT – Battle the rain past creeks, mangroves and refinery flares.
• KANO – Drive through the ancient city gates and the Kofar Mata dye pits in the Harmattan haze.
• EKITI – Climb and dive over misty green hills to Ikogosi Warm Springs.
• ENUGU – Tackle the bends of Milliken Hill as the sun sets over Coal City.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
• Career – Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next city.
• Quick Race – You against five rival drivers.
• Traffic Rush – Endless traffic. Score big with near misses — but three crashes and you're done.
• Time Trial – Reach every checkpoint before the clock runs out.

YOUR WAY TO DRIVE
Tilt your phone to steer, or use the on-screen buttons — or both at once. Hit N₂O for a burst of speed and sound your horn at slow okadas.

NAIJA ROADS, NAIJA RULES
Dodge danfos, okadas, kekes, tankers and lorries. Watch out for potholes, and slow down at police checkpoints — overspeeding attracts a fine!

GARAGE
Earn naira to buy five cars, from the Tokunbo Sedan to the Super Eagle GT. Upgrade the engine, nitro and tyres, and paint your ride.

Original Afrobeat-style soundtrack. Plays fully offline. No sign-up needed.
